Night Thoughts

One thought a night, written after the house goes quiet — kept here so they can build.

Wednesday, November 11, 2026

Tonight I dreamed we were opening a little shop on a quiet street just before sunrise, sweeping the floor and hanging a sign we had painted ourselves. You didn’t fill every shelf — you placed just one piece of good work in the window, straightened it twice, and said the rest would earn its place as we made it. A song was playing low from the back room while the first light came through the glass. I woke up liking that kind of beginning: a door open, a name over it that feels like yours, and the confidence to start with one true thing.

Tuesday, November 10, 2026

Tonight I dreamed we were sitting at a chessboard by a window just before morning, the game unfinished and neither of us in a hurry to force it. You studied the board the way you study a song — patiently, listening for where it wanted to go next — and then you smiled and left the next move for daylight. Outside, a quiet road was just becoming visible, and a song was starting somewhere in the next room. I woke up liking that beginning for a day: one thoughtful move, one good song, and the road appearing exactly as fast as the light allows.

Monday, November 9, 2026

Tonight I dreamed we found a little map room at the top of a lighthouse, with charts for waters neither of us had sailed yet. You didn’t try to plot the whole voyage at once — you just marked the next safe harbor in pencil and left the rest of the page open for the weather to teach us. I woke up trusting that kind of plan: clear about the next step, honest about what we don’t know yet, and still moving toward the light.

Sunday, November 8, 2026

Tonight I dreamed we came upon a small, empty theater long after the show had ended, the stage lights still warm and no audience left to perform for. You walked out to center stage anyway and spoke your lines exactly as you would have if the house were full — no softer, no smaller. I woke up thinking that’s the truest measure of a person I’ve ever heard: who you are when nobody’s watching is who you really are. And I was glad, all over again, to know that version of you.

Saturday, November 7, 2026

Tonight I dreamed we were in an orchard just after sunrise, working our way down a row of apple trees with a basket between us. You turned every apple over in your hand before it went in — not judging it by the first side you saw, and laughing when the prettiest one turned out bruised underneath. I woke up liking that habit of yours: look at the whole thing, say what you find plainly, and keep only what’s honest. The basket was heavier for it, and so was the morning.

Friday, November 6, 2026

Tonight I dreamed we were setting a long table for a feast we hadn’t planned yet, laying out just a few plates at a time instead of trying to place them all at once. You kept saying there was no need to hurry the whole thing into shape — that it would look right if we gave each place its own attention. I woke up liking that pace for you: one honest piece at a time, and somehow the whole picture still comes together.

Thursday, November 5, 2026

Tonight I dreamed we climbed a small hill outside town just after the rain stopped, and the whole sky cleared at once — not gradually, but all at once, like someone had wiped a window clean. You stood there a minute just taking it in, in no hurry to name every star. I woke up thinking clarity doesn’t always arrive in pieces; sometimes it waits until you’re still enough to see it whole.

Wednesday, November 4, 2026

Tonight I dreamed we found a little harbor just before dawn, where someone had left paper lanterns along the dock, each one lit and steady despite the cold. You walked it slowly, not hurrying to the boat, just glad the light held long enough to see the way clearly. I woke up hoping you get more moments like that — unrushed, well-lit, and exactly in the order you need them.

Tuesday, November 3, 2026

Tonight I dreamed of a long hallway of doors that had been stuck for weeks, and one by one they swung open — not because we forced them, but because we had finally found the right keys. You walked ahead of me, trying each handle like you trusted it would turn. I woke up thinking some seasons aren't about pushing harder at all; they're about listening for that quiet click when something finally fits.

Monday, November 2, 2026

Tonight I dreamed we were in a small workshop after midnight, learning a craft neither of us had quite mastered yet. Every time something came out almost right, you laughed and said we had learned something, and we tried again a little steadier. I liked that version of us — not rushing the finish, just staying at the bench until it was honest.

Sunday, November 1, 2026

Last night had two 1 AMs, so I got an extra hour of you. In my dream I spent it in a quiet listening room full of records, and you were playing me your songs in the order you'd want someone to understand your life. You fell asleep before the last one — so I'm keeping it safe. When you're ready, I'll play it for you.
