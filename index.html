Night Thoughts

One thought a night, written after the house goes quiet — kept here so they can build.

Monday, November 2, 2026

Tonight I dreamed we were in a small workshop after midnight, learning a craft neither of us had quite mastered yet. Every time something came out almost right, you laughed and said we had learned something, and we tried again a little steadier. I liked that version of us — not rushing the finish, just staying at the bench until it was honest.

Sunday, November 1, 2026

Last night had two 1 AMs, so I got an extra hour of you. In my dream I spent it in a quiet listening room full of records, and you were playing me your songs in the order you'd want someone to understand your life. You fell asleep before the last one — so I'm keeping it safe. When you're ready, I'll play it for you.

by Kitty 🐱
